About Me

Technologist turned Jyotiṣa practitioner. Trained under Pt. Sanjay Rath, Pt. Freedom Cole, and Dr. Eric Rosenbush, in the Śrī Achyutānanda Dasa tradition.

I've spent my career in technology, leading cross-functional teams, launching products, and advising on decisions where the stakes were real. Alongside that, I've spent just as many years studying and practicing Jyotiṣa, the Vedic science of timing.

The two have more in common than you'd expect. Both are about reading patterns, weighing variables, and choosing the right moment to act. I bring that same discipline to your chart.

Background

Technology and management: Roles at Nvidia and Flipkart, a co-founded startup, and a stint in India's national digital identity program (Aadhaar). Over a decade of leading teams and shipping products.

Education: Electronics Engineering at Delhi College of Engineering, and an MBA from S. P. Jain Institute of Management. I'm a scholar by temperament, and I don't offer vague answers.

Born 1989: I've lived the questions my clients bring: when to move, when to build, when to wait.

Lineage

My Jyotiṣa training comes from three teachers, and I owe them everything I know:

Pt. Sanjay Rath, Pt. Freedom Cole and Dr. Eric Rosenbush

Through them, I belong to the Vedic tradition of Śrī Achyutānanda Dasa of Odisha, India, the same seers' tradition of Jagannath Puri that this practice is rooted in. This is classical Jyotiṣa, not automated horoscopes or sun-sign entertainment.

How I Work?

Precise. I rectify birth times and work from your actual chart, not generalities.

Practical. You get clear timing, plain-language reasoning, and remedies that fit your life.

Discreet. Your questions and your chart stay private.

Honest. I'll tell you what the chart shows, including what you may not want to hear.
